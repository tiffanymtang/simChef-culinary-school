This figure compares interaction recovery across interaction orders and training sample sizes. The x-axis gives interaction order (2 through 5), the y-axis gives mean recovery across the 20 replicates, and grouped bars correspond to $n\in\{400,1200,2000\}$. A height of 1 means that every true interaction of that order was recovered in every replicate; a height of 0 means none were recovered.
